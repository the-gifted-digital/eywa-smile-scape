Section 02

Our Approach

Why this is built differently from typical SEO

We don't do keyword-first SEO. We build a semantic, evidence-first content system — structured so that Google, Bing and AI assistants understand SmileScape's expertise at the deepest possible level. It is the same principle used by the world's leading medical institutions, adapted for the Thai dental market through our proprietary content methodology.

⚠️
Why medical SEO is different. Dental and health content falls under Google's strictest quality category — YMYL (Your Money or Your Life). Every page is held to a far higher standard of accuracy, authority and trust than any other industry. Our architecture is built around that reality: real doctors, real evidence, real outcomes.
