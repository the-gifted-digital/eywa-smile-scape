## Patterns Observed Across Competitors

### Common sitemap section names (Thai dental vertical norms)

- รากฟันเทียม / ทำรากฟันเทียม / รากเทียม
- จัดฟัน / จัดฟันใส
- ทำฟัน (catch-all for general restorative)
- ทีมแพทย์ / หมอ
- โปรโมชั่น (prominent — price-driven market)
- บทความ / ความรู้ / ข้อมูล
- สาขา / ติดต่อ

### Common URL structures

- /รากฟันเทียม/ or /service/implant/ or /dental-implant/
- /branch/{slug}/ or /สาขา/{name}/
- /blog/{slug}/ or /ความรู้/{slug}/
- /promotion/ or /โปรโมชั่น/

### Common hero CTAs

- "นัดปรึกษาฟรี" / "ปรึกษาทันตแพทย์ฟรี"
- "ดูโปรโมชั่น" / "ดูราคา"
- "Add LINE @" (ubiquitous in Thai market)
- "จองคิวออนไลน์"

### Common authority displays

- Doctor count ("ทันตแพทย์ XX คน")
- Case count ("ผ่าน X,XXX เคส")
- Years established ("ประสบการณ์ XX ปี")
- International brand names (Straumann, Osstem, NeoBiotech)
- Near-transit messaging ("ใกล้ MRT", "มีที่จอดรถ")

### Common schema gaps (inferred from market norms — verify per site)

- FAQPage still widely emitted despite DR-019 deprecation for rich results
- Most Thai dental clinic sites: Organization schema, not MedicalBusiness or Dentist sub-type
- AggregateRating rarely structured — reviews kept on Facebook/Google only
- No reviewedBy on YMYL service pages (standard gap across Thai health verticals)
- MedicalProcedure schema on service pages: rare

## Positioning Opportunity Map

### Where competitors cluster (avoid me-too positioning)

- **Price-led standard implants at 25,000-35,000 THB** — SmileSeasons, LDC, Smile Signature all crowd this zone with chain-style delivery
- **Generic "digital implant" claims** — most larger clinics now claim digital workflow without differentiating what that means
- **NeoBiotech / Osstem brand mentions** — Korean implants used as price-point signals without clinical depth

### White space (SmileScape can own)

- **Implant-first specialist clinic with named doctor pedigree** — No chain competitor can claim "studied with Dr. Istvan Urban in Hungary" or "trained at ILAPEO Brazil." This is category-exclusive.
- **Lifetime warranty vs 10-year** — LDC's 10-year is the best competitor offer found. SmileScape's lifetime is a clear head-to-head win — needs prominent page placement.
- **Sausage Technique (vertical bone defect)** — No Bangkok clinic content found on this technique. Full white space for educational pillar + procedure page.
- **All-on-X / immediate loading complex cases** — NP Digital covers digital implant but not complex arch reconstruction. SmileScape's ILAPEO training is ownable.
- **Ceramic / metal-free implant option** — Niche but growing segment. SmileScape lists it in brand config. No clear competitor found owning this space in Bangkok.
- **Dual-branch geography** — รัตนาธิเบศร์ + ศรีนครินทร์ combo. SmileSeasons doesn't cover either area. LDC covers รัตนาธิเบศร์ but not as implant-first.
- **Family-warmth + ethics positioning** — "ถ้าเคสนี้เราไม่กล้าทำให้พ่อแม่ตัวเอง..." — no chain competitor can make this credible.

### Layer 2 blog topics competitors do well (SmileScape should match or exceed)

- Implant price comparison articles (SmileSeasons leading with roundup content)
- General "what is implant" educational content (commodity — needs depth differentiation)
- "จัดฟัน vs รากฟันเทียม" decision content

### Layer 2 blog topics competitors miss (gap SmileScape can fill)

- Sausage Technique educational content (zero coverage found in Thai market)
- Ceramic implant comparison (very thin coverage)
- Vertical bone defect / severe bone loss cases — complex case education
- All-on-X journey content in Thai (ILAPEO methodology not documented by any Bangkok clinic)
- Post-implant maintenance depth content (long-tail, low competition)
- Implant + orthodontic sequencing (interdisciplinary cases)
